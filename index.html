Privacy Policy

EVA AI by Alamgir App Studio

Effective date: November 17, 2026 · Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how EVA AI (the "App"), published by Alamgir App Studio ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the App on your Android device. We respect your privacy. The App has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and we do not operate a server that stores your content.

By using the App you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Summary

  • Your chats, memories, saved results, agents, workspaces and folders are stored only on your device.
  • To create an AI answer, the content you submit is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to third-party AI services (see section 5).
  • We do not have accounts, sell your data, show ads, use advertising IDs, or run analytics or crash-reporting tools inside the App.
  • You can back up and delete your data yourself at any time.

3. Information the App handles

3.1 Content you create or choose

  • Chat messages and prompts you type or speak, and AI answers.
  • Chat Memory notes and Custom Instructions that you write.
  • PDF files you choose: the App reads the text inside the PDF on your device.
  • Images you choose (for image understanding, screenshot analysis, OCR and image-to-prompt). The App makes a smaller copy of the image in memory for sending.
  • Saved results, custom AI agents, workspaces, prompts and folders that you create.

3.2 Voice features

When you use voice features, the App asks for the microphone permission. Speech recognition is performed by the speech recognition service on your phone (usually provided by Google or your device maker), which may process audio on-device or on its servers under its own privacy policy. The App itself does not record, store or upload audio files. Only the recognized text is used by the App. Text to speech uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine.

3.3 Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The App receives only whether your subscription is active, and keeps that status on your device so the App works if you are briefly offline.

3.4 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, device advertising ID, photos or files other than the ones you choose to use with an AI feature, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

5. Third-party AI services (important)

The App does not run its own AI model. To answer your request, the App sends the following to one or more AI services, over HTTPS:

  • your current message or the text/prompt of the tool you use;
  • up to the last 10 messages of the current conversation (for context);
  • your Chat Memory notes and Custom Instructions (when Chat Memory is on);
  • text extracted from PDFs you choose (up to about 20,000 characters per request, up to 24,000 for translation);
  • images you choose, for image features.

The App tries these services in turn (and may switch automatically if one is busy or at its limit):

6. How we use information

  • To provide the App's features and generate AI answers, summaries, translations and documents.
  • To remember your preferences (Chat Memory, Custom Instructions, reply language).
  • To verify that your subscription is active.
  • To keep a local cache of answers to common questions so the App is faster and uses fewer AI requests.

We do not use your content for advertising, profiling or selling to anyone.

7. Storage, backup and retention

  • On-device storage: chats, memory notes, saved results, agents, workspaces, folders, settings and a small answer cache are stored in the App's private storage on your device. They stay until you delete them or uninstall the App. The answer cache is cleared automatically after about 30 days (and you can clear it in Settings).
  • Backup and restore: you can create a backup file (.evab) and save it where you choose. This file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. We cannot access or recover it. The App disables Android's automatic cloud backup for its data.
  • Export Center: files you export (PDF, DOCX, TXT) are saved only in the location you pick.
  • Pictures and PDFs you choose are not copied into the App's storage. They are processed in memory for the request.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete chats, memories, agents, workspaces and saved results inside the App, or delete everything by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android settings.
  • Turn Chat Memory off or edit it any time. Clear Custom Instructions in Settings.
  • Deny the microphone permission in Android settings and still use the other features.
  • Cancel your subscription in Google Play > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions.

Because we do not keep your content on our own servers, we cannot look up or delete it for you. For content already sent to an AI service, please contact that service using the links in section 5. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Brazil or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Contact us at the email below with any privacy request and we will help as far as we can.

9. Security

Requests to AI services and to Google Play use encrypted HTTPS connections. Data on your device is stored in the App's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Protect your device with a screen lock.

Answers are generated by artificial intelligence and may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated or inappropriate. They are for general information and creative help only and are not professional medical, legal, financial or safety advice. Please verify important information. You are responsible for how you use the output.
